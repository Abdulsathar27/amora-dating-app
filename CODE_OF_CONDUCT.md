# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a friendly, harassment-free place for everyone,
whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/Abdulsathar27/amora-dating-app/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the maintainer [@Abdulsathar27](https://github.com/Abdulsathar27).
Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that break this code.
